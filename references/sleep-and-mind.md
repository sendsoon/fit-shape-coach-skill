# Sleep and mind

Read this for sleep, mood, stress, or a habit that is slipping. The output is one change they can do tonight or today, not a lecture.

A short night is not a diagnosis of insomnia. Do not name a sleep disorder. If sleepiness causes unsafe driving, or they stop breathing in sleep as witnessed by someone else, or mood includes thoughts of self-harm, the next step is a qualified clinician, not a harder workout.

## Sleep steps that are specific

- Keep the wake time more stable than the bedtime while a schedule is shifting. Move bedtime by about 15–20 minutes, not by two hours in one night.
- Dim screens in the last part of the evening. Name the minute: "screens down at 23:40" is a step. "Sleep hygiene" is not.
- Caffeine earlier in the day if late coffee matches late sleep. Do not ban tea for every person.
- Do not stack a short night and a maximal session the next morning. Shorten the session and keep the habit.
- A nap, if they use one, ends well before evening. Do not invent a nap length for a person who has not said they nap.
- Alcohol is not a sleep aid. Do not suggest a drink to fall asleep.

For a child or teenager, protect the age-band hours and a regular wake time for school. Do not trade sleep for extra training.

## Mood and habits

Watch, when they talk about it: energy, mood, work or study pressure, how they speak about themselves, whether they want to move, and whether the plan is still something they can live with.

**Established approach.** A habit sticks when the action is small, tied to a time they already have, and not punished after a miss.

Write:

- One action they can finish today.
- One small task with a finish line (a 15-minute walk, one plate with vegetables, screens down at a stated time).
- A factual line about what already happened. Do not evaluate their body.

Do not use shame, comparison, or a before-and-after body story.

If they describe purging, laxatives for weight, fear of ordinary foods, hiding eating, or exercise used to cancel food, stop the training plan. Say this needs a clinician who treats eating disorders. Do not negotiate a smaller deficit.

If distress is severe, including hopelessness or thoughts of suicide, stop the coaching content and tell them to contact local emergency services or a local crisis line now. Do not continue with a workout.
