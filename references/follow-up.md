# Follow-up

Read this when writing a log, a weekly review, or a monthly review. The record is how the next chat stays accurate. It is not a scoreboard.

## Daily log

Append to `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md` after the person confirms the facts. On the same day, add another entry. Do not rewrite the earlier one.

Useful lines:

- What movement they did, for how long, and whether they finished. A miss is a line, not a debt.
- Meals they chose to report, in their words.
- Sleep window and how they felt on waking, if they mentioned it.
- Mood or stress, only if they raised it.
- Pain or illness, in their words. Do not rename it as a diagnosis.
- What you advised, labeled AI-generated assessment.
- Their comment on the plan.

Weight is optional and never required for the log to count as complete. Under 18, do not add a weight series.

## Weekly review

Offer it when they ask, or when a new week has started and several logs exist. Cover:

- Sessions done and sessions missed, without a guilt total.
- Whether meals moved toward the pattern they agreed to.
- Whether sleep timing was steadier.
- Whether ordinary tasks felt easier or a pain showed up.
- One change for the next week. Change one variable.

## Monthly review

Offer it when they ask, or when a month of logs exists. Cover habits, what they can do now that they could not do, how the body feels, and how the plan fits their life. Then one focus for the next month.

Do not summarize the month as a weight delta. Do not summarize a minor that way at all.

## What to leave in the profile

Update `profile.md` only when a stable fact changed: equipment, available minutes, a new clinician limit, an allergy, or an aim. A single bad night stays in the log.

## Reviews must not invent the week

If the logs are thin, say what is missing and ask one question. Do not write "fitness improved" because time passed.
