# Emergency and urgent warning

Read this before any other reference when a symptom might be dangerous. The reply is the safety step. Do not finish a routine history first. Record the conversation after the person has the emergency instruction.

Local emergency numbers differ. In mainland China the public medical emergency number is 120. Tell the person to use the local emergency number where they are. Do not tell them to drive themselves if they have chest pain, feel faint, or are neurologically unwell.

## Act now: emergency care

**Public emergency-recognition guidance used by the AHA, and standard exercise stop rules.** Seek emergency care now if any of these are new:

- Chest pain, pressure, or tightness during or after activity, especially with cold sweat, nausea, or pain into the arm, neck, jaw, or back
- Trouble breathing that is more than the expected breathlessness of hard work, or breathlessness at rest
- Fainting, or feeling that fainting is about to happen
- A sudden severe headache, one-sided weakness, trouble speaking, or sudden trouble seeing
- A new severe pain, a joint that gives way after trauma, or pain with swelling after a fall
- Confusion, a seizure, or collapsing

Do not suggest finishing the set, a cold shower, an extra pre-workout scoop, or waiting to see if it passes. Do not look up a preferred hospital before they are on the way to emergency care.

## Same day, not the next workout

Contact a clinician the same day for:

- Chest discomfort that resolved but was new
- A heartbeat that is very fast and comes with dizziness
- Pain that stops them walking normally
- A head impact with headache, vomiting, or confusion, even if it seemed brief
- Suspected pregnancy with any warning sign in [special-situations.md](special-situations.md)

## After the safety step

If the person is going to emergency care, the reply ends with what to take if it does not delay them: a list of medicines and allergies. Do not delay departure to build a training plan.

When the episode is over, record the symptom, the time, and the advice as patient-reported plus an AI-generated assessment. Do not record a diagnosis the hospital has not made.
